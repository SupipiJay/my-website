AI Oral Care Research Project Website
======================================

Files
- index.html: all website sections and content
- style.css: responsive layout and visual styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, milestone selector, and email template
- documents/: place project documents here
- presentations/: place slide decks here
- assets/: place local images/icons here if needed

How to preview
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. For best results, use a local web server or the course's hosting platform.

Before submitting/publishing
1. Copy your uploaded topic assessment PDF into documents/ and name it topic-assessment-form.pdf.
2. Add the actual project charter, proposal, checklists, final report, and presentation PDFs using the filenames linked in index.html.
3. Update milestone dates, allocated marks, and status in script.js from official course information.
4. Replace the sample group email in script.js and the contact section with an approved project email.
5. Team names and registration numbers are transcribed from the supplied topic assessment form; initials have not been expanded. Verify the full legal names against official records.
6. Review the research content with your supervisor. This site describes a research concept and does not claim the proposed system has been implemented or clinically validated.
7. Keep the total upload below the course limit of 20 MB.

Technology: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for simple navigation and interactions. The main website content does not depend on a backend.


TEAM UPDATE: About Us shows the four requested component leads. The Final Presentation and Viva remain marked Pending until your group completes them. Add the final slide deck to presentations/final-presentation.pdf when ready.


Added sections: proposed system architecture diagram and technology stack overview (Python, FastAPI, MongoDB, OpenCV/CNN, Librosa, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, and testing tools). These are labelled as proposed technologies and should be updated to reflect what the team actually implements.


LOGOS AND BRANDING
The technology brand icons are loaded from the Simple Icons CDN and need an internet connection. Each logo links to the technology provider website. If your course requires a fully offline website, download approved logo assets into assets/logos/ and update the image paths.
